I've added `tests/test_report.py` with 47 tests for `summarise` and `format_summary`. The whole suite passes (92 tests) and `ruff check` is clean. Nothing under `src/` changed. All 47 pass against the current code, so the tests didn't find any bugs in `report.py`.

**`summarise`**
- **Distance:** great-circle distance on the README's 6371.0088 km sphere, rounded to 0.1 km.
- **Ascent and descent:** small jitter adds nothing, a 300 m climb in 1 m steps counts in full, totals are rounded to 0.1 m, the `threshold` argument is honoured, and a negative threshold raises `ValueError`.
- **Highest and lowest points.**
- **Steepest grade:** the README's 18 m over 100 m example gives 18.0, downhill legs count, and repeated points are skipped.
- **Walking time (Naismith's rule):** 5 km/h on the flat plus an hour per 600 m of ascent; descent adds nothing; `fitness` divides the time; zero or negative fitness raises `ValueError`.
- **Time isn't rounded early:** a 1.04 km track shows 1.0 km but is timed as 1.04 km.
- **Matches the day plan:** the days from `plan.split_days` add up to the summary time, at two fitness values.
- **Heading:** all eight compass points, measured from start to finish only; `loop` when the finish is within 0.1 km of the start, and a compass point just beyond that.
- **Other:** `Summary` can't be modified after creation, and a track recorded in feet is summarised in metres.

**`format_summary`**
- The exact seven-line layout, and how numbers are rounded for display.
- Durations to the nearest minute, using the README's examples (`5h 07m`, `0h 45m`, `12h 00m`) plus edge cases such as 2h 59.6m showing as `3h 00m`.
- An end-to-end check from track text to the printed summary.

The tests work out expected values on their own: the README's Earth radius gives the degrees-per-km conversion, rather than the tests calling the package's geometry helpers.

I left one case untested: a time landing exactly on half a minute. The README only says "nearest minute", and Python's `round()` rounds exact halves to the even number, so the right answer there isn't defined.
