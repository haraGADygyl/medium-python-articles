Tobler is available as `pace="tobler"` on `pace.leg_hours`, `plan.split_days` and `report.summarise`, and as `--pace {naismith,tobler}` on both CLI commands. Naismith stays the default and its output is unchanged. The changes are uncommitted in the working tree.

**Verification**
- 65 tests pass (47 existing, 18 new). ruff check, ruff format and mypy are clean.
- I captured `summary` and `plan` output on a sample track before the change and diffed it afterwards: identical.
- The bad-pace case fails from the CLI with argparse's exit 2 and an "invalid choice" message. From the API it raises `ValueError`.

**What changed**
- `pace.py`: `check_pace` plus a `pace` argument on `leg_hours`. Under Tobler each leg takes `length / tobler_kmh(grade)` hours, divided by fitness, with grade taken from the raw elevation difference of the leg. Zero-length legs take no time.
- `report.py`: under Tobler, the summary time is the sum of the leg times. Naismith still uses the existing formula.
- `plan.py`: `split_days` passes `pace` through to `leg_hours`.
- `cli.py`: `--pace` on `summary` and `plan`, restricted to the two choices.
- `README.md`: the walking-time, pace, Tobler and day-plan sections are updated.

**Choices I made**
- Under Tobler the ascent threshold does not affect time. The summary's ascent and descent figures still use it.
- Under Tobler the day plan's legs sum exactly to the summary time, the same guarantee Naismith gives.

**Known limit:** a leg whose elevation change is more than about 213 times its horizontal length (for example, a 213 m rise over 1 m) makes `tobler_kmh` underflow to 0, which raises `ZeroDivisionError`. I left the literal formula as specified. If you want such legs handled, I can add a guard.
