I added `tests/test_elevation.py` with 30 tests; the full suite passes (70 tests) and `ruff check tests` is clean. Nothing under `src/` was touched, and the file is not committed.

The tests found no discrepancies between `elevation.py` and the README or docstrings. They cover:

- **`gain_and_loss`**:
  - the 3 m default threshold, and that a change of exactly the threshold counts;
  - one-metre jitter adding nothing;
  - a 300 m climb or descent in 1 m steps counting in full;
  - changes measured from the last counted elevation;
  - custom and zero thresholds;
  - rounding to 0.1 m;
  - empty, single-point and flat inputs;
  - `ValueError` on a negative threshold.
- **`highest_and_lowest`**: an ordinary track, a level track, and elevations below sea level.
- **`steepest_grade`**:
  - the README's example of 18 m over 100 m giving `18.0`;
  - downhill legs reported as positive;
  - the steepest single leg winning in either direction;
  - rounding to 0.1;
  - a level track giving zero;
  - repeated points skipped, including a track made only of repeated points.

Two of the tested behaviours are my reading of the docs rather than something they state outright:

- **Downhill grade is positive**: "uphill or downhill" doesn't say whether a descent is signed, and the tests assert the magnitude.
- **All-repeated-points track gives `0.0`**: the docs only say zero-length legs are skipped.
