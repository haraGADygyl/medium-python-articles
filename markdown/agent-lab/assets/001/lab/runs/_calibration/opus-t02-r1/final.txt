I've written `tests/test_pace.py` with 34 tests for `src/trailhead/pace.py`, and they all pass. The full suite (74 tests) passes too, and `ruff check` and `ruff format` are clean. Nothing under `src/` was changed, and none of the tests turned up a difference between the code and what the README and docstrings say.

What the tests check:

- **`naismith_hours`:**
  - 5 km/h on the flat, plus one hour per 600 m of ascent, and the two add up.
  - Times aren't rounded.
  - `fitness` divides the time (1.25 is faster, 0.8 slower) and defaults to 1.0.
  - Zero or negative fitness, negative distance and negative ascent each raise `ValueError`.
- **`tobler_kmh`:**
  - It matches `6 * exp(-3.5 * |grade + 0.05|)` across a range of grades.
  - The top speed is 6 km/h at a 5% descent, and nothing goes faster.
  - Speed is the same at equal distances either side of that 5% descent.
  - Steeper climbs are slower, and a 10% descent is faster than a 10% climb.
- **`leg_hours`:**
  - It returns one time per leg, and each leg counts only its own climb. An up–down–up track gives `[1.0, 0.0, 0.5]`.
  - Descent adds nothing.
  - `fitness` is applied, and bad fitness raises `ValueError`.
  - For a steady climb, the leg times add up to `naismith_hours` for the whole track.

Most tests use legs along the equator, so leg lengths are exact. One test uses the README's Rila coordinates instead and checks the result against `Track.leg_lengths_km()`.
