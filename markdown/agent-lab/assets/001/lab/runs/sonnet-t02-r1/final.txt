I added `tests/test_pace.py` with 26 tests. All of them pass and `ruff check` is clean. I didn't change anything under `src/`.

The tests cover `pace.py` as the README and docstrings describe it:

- **`naismith_hours`**:
  - 5 km/h on the flat, plus one hour per 600 m of ascent.
  - `fitness` divides the time.
  - The result isn't rounded.
  - A negative distance or ascent raises `ValueError`.
  - A zero or negative `fitness` raises `ValueError`.
- **`tobler_kmh`**:
  - The peak is 6 km/h at a −5% grade.
  - Values match `6*exp(-3.5*|g+0.05|)`.
  - The curve is symmetric about −5%.
  - Descending is faster than climbing at the same steepness.
  - Steeper slopes are slower.
- **`leg_hours`**:
  - It returns one value per leg.
  - A climb is credited to the leg where it's counted.
  - Descent adds nothing.
  - Jitter under the 3 m threshold is ignored.
  - A climb in 1 m steps still counts in full.
  - `fitness` is applied.
  - A bad `fitness` raises `ValueError`.
  - The legs sum to the summary time (`naismith_hours` on total distance and counted ascent) at fitness 1.0, 1.25 and 0.8.

The tests turned up no discrepancies between the code and the docs.
