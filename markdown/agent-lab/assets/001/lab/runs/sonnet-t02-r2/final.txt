I wrote `tests/test_pace.py` with 32 tests, and they all pass. I didn't touch anything under `src/`. I didn't run `ruff check` on the new file.

The tests cover the behaviour in the README and docstrings:

- **`naismith_hours`:**
  - Flat speed is 5 km/h, plus one hour per 600 m of ascent.
  - `fitness` divides the time, and the default is 1.0.
  - The result isn't rounded.
  - Zero or negative fitness raises `ValueError`.
  - Negative distance or ascent raises `ValueError`.
- **`tobler_kmh`:**
  - It peaks at 6 km/h on a 5% descent.
  - It matches the formula on flat ground, at a 10% climb and at a 10% descent.
  - It is symmetric around the peak and never goes above 6.
  - Grade is a fraction, not a percent.
- **`leg_hours`:**
  - It returns one value per leg.
  - Climb is credited to the leg where it counts under the 3 m threshold.
  - Jitter adds nothing, and small steps are credited once they reach the threshold.
  - Descent adds nothing.
  - `fitness` is applied, and invalid fitness raises `ValueError`.
  - Zero-length legs take no time.
  - The legs add up to the summary time (`naismith_hours` of total length and `gain_and_loss` ascent) at several fitness values.
