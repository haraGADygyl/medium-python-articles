I've written `tests/test_plan.py` with 24 tests, and they all pass. The full suite passes too (66 tests), `ruff check` and `mypy` are clean, and I didn't touch anything under `src/`.

The tests check these things, taken from the README and docstrings:
- **`split_days` structure:**
  - Days are numbered from 1.
  - The first day starts at point 0 and the last ends at the final point.
  - Each day starts where the previous one ended.
- **Day limit:**
  - A day never goes over the limit.
  - Legs are packed greedily.
  - A leg that exactly fills the limit stays in its day.
  - A single leg longer than the limit becomes a day by itself, including when it's the first leg.
- **Timing:**
  - Hours are rounded to 0.01.
  - Climbing adds time and descent adds none.
  - `fitness` shortens days and divides the total time.
  - The days add up to the summary time on a track with jittery elevations.
- **Invalid input:** `ValueError` for a limit or fitness of zero or below.
- **`describe_plan`:**
  - The format matches `Day 2: points 14-31, 6h 05m`.
  - Durations cover `5h 07m`, `0h 45m`, `12h 00m` and a round-up to `2h 00m`.
  - It returns one line per day, and an empty list for no days.

I found no discrepancies between `plan.py` and its documentation.
