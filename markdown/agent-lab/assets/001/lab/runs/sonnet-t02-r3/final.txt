I added `tests/test_pace.py` with 21 tests. They all pass and `ruff check` is clean. I didn't touch anything under `src/`. I didn't run `mypy`.

The tests check these behaviours from the README and docstrings:
- **`naismith_hours`:**
  - 5 km/h on the flat, plus 1 hour per 600 m of ascent, added together.
  - `fitness` divides the time, for both 1.25 and 0.8.
  - The result isn't rounded.
  - Zero or negative fitness, negative distance and negative ascent each raise `ValueError`.
- **`tobler_kmh`:** the peak of 6 km/h at a 5% descent, the exact formula on the flat, climbs and descents, and a climb being slower than the same descent.
- **`leg_hours`:**
  - It returns one value per leg.
  - Climb is credited to the leg where it counts.
  - Descent adds nothing.
  - 1 m of jitter is ignored.
  - A long climb in 1 m steps still counts.
  - `fitness` is applied.
  - The leg times add up to `naismith_hours` on the track's total distance and `gain_and_loss` ascent, at three fitness values.
