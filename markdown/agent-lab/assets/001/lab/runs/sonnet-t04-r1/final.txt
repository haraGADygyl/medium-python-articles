I added `tests/test_plan.py` with 31 tests. The full suite passes (76 tests), and `ruff check` and `ruff format --check` are clean on the new file. I didn't touch anything under `src/`.

They cover the behaviour in the README and docstrings:

- **`split_days`:**
  - A short track is one day, and days split between legs.
  - Day numbers start at 1, point indexes start at 0, and consecutive days share a boundary point.
  - Hours are rounded to 0.01, and no day goes over the limit unless a single leg does.
  - A leg that is over the limit on its own gets its own day, including when it is the first leg.
  - Climbs count toward a day's time and descents add nothing.
  - GPS jitter under the 3 m threshold adds no climb, and a 600 m climb in 1 m steps still counts in full.
  - `fitness` changes the number of days.
  - A limit of zero or less, or a fitness of zero or less, raises `ValueError`.
  - The days of a plan add up to the Naismith time for the whole track, within rounding, at several fitness levels.
- **`describe_plan`:**
  - It gives one line per day, in the docstring format.
  - The README durations come out as `5h 07m`, `0h 45m` and `12h 00m`.
  - Minutes round to the nearest and carry into the hour (`1.999` gives `2h 00m`), and an empty plan gives no lines.
